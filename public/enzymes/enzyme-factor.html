Key points
Low temperature: low activity but enzymes usually do not denature (activity can recover). High temperature: denaturation — activity cannot recover. Extreme pH also breaks hydrogen bonds and destroys the enzyme's shape. Different enzymes have different optimum pH (e.g. pepsin in the stomach vs intestinal enzymes).
