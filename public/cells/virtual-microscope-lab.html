Exam tips

Total magnification = eyepiece magnification × objective magnification (e.g. 10× eyepiece × 4× objective = 40×).

Inverted image — what you see under a light microscope is upside-down and reversed left-to-right.

Focus order — start with the lowest objective, use the coarse knob, then the fine knob. Increase magnification only after the image is sharp.
