Question • Difficulty: Hard

Describe the role of NAD+ in the process of Glycolysis.

Tap or space to reveal answer

Answer • Concept: Energy Carriers

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+) acts as an electron carrier (oxidizing agent).

  • Accepts 2 electrons and a hydrogen ion to become NADH.
  • Transfers these high-energy electrons to the Electron Transport Chain.
  • Essential for maintaining the continuous cycle of ATP production in the cytoplasm.

Exam Tip: Distinguish between NAD+ and NADP+ (Photosynthesis) to avoid losing marks!
