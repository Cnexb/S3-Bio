DEFINITION

Osmosis is the net movement of water molecules from a region of higher water potential to a region of lower water potential across a differentially permeable membrane.

Plasmolysis

The shrinking of protoplasm away from the cell wall when placed in a hypertonic solution.

Turgidity

The state of a plant cell being swollen and firm due to high internal water pressure.

warning EXAM PITFALL

Never say "concentration of water". Always use the term Water Potential (Ψ) to secure full marks in Paper 1.

Why It Matters for DSE

Most students struggle with the distinction between animal and plant cell responses. While plant cells have a rigid cell wall to prevent bursting, animal cells (like red blood cells) will undergo haemolysis in distilled water.
