Diffusion 擴散作用 · Definition

Definition: Net movement high → low concentration until equilibrium.

Molecules still move both ways — only the net direction matters.

Diffusion · Factors & Fig 8

Greater gradient — e.g. intestine; capillaries remove products
Larger surface area — e.g. microvilli
Shorter distance — thin, one-cell-thick walls
Higher temperature — more kinetic energy
Smaller & non-polar — CO₂, O₂ faster than glucose

Osmosis 滲透作用 · Definition

Osmosis = net movement of water across differentially permeable 差異滲透性 membrane from higher ψ to lower ψ.
水分子穿過差異滲透膜，從水勢較高向水勢較低的淨移動
Requirements:
  1. Differentially permeable membrane
  2. Water potential difference
Water follows solute! 水跟溶質走

Passive — no energy required.

Osmosis · Water potential ψ 水勢

RegionSoluteWater potential ψ
Less solute (dilute)Lower solute conc.Higher ψ (pure water ≈ 0)
More solute (concentrated)Higher solute conc.Lower ψ (more negative)

Solute does not cross the membrane in osmosis — only water net movement. Equal ψ → osmosis stops.

Applied · Digestive system 消化系統

Active transport 主動運輸

Uses energy from respiration (ATP) to change carrier protein shape — often against the concentration gradient.

Example: absorption of nitrates into root hair cells when soil concentration is low.

Phagocytosis 吞噬作用

Engulfing large particles — membrane folding enabled by fluidity. Cargo: proteins & large particles (not glucose, O₂, small ions).

1Infolding → pseudopodia 偽足
2Particle engulfed
3Enclosed in vacuole 液泡
4Lysosome 溶酶體 fuses → digest
5Products diffuse into cytoplasm
Examples: Amoeba feeding; WBC engulfing bacteria
NOT phagocytosis: O₂ transport; intestinal glucose absorption

Water moves toward the solution with lower ψ (usually higher solute).

True / False Set 3 — ✔ if correct · ✘ on wrong words

11. In osmosis, water moves towards the region with lower solute concentration.

✘ False 錯誤

Water moves toward higher solute concentration (lower ψ).

12. The net movement of water in osmosis is from low to high solute concentration.

✔ True 正確

Low solute = high water potential; water moves from dilute to concentrated regions (high ψ → low ψ).

13. Osmosis stops when there is no water potential difference.

✔ True 正確

At equilibrium, net movement of water is zero.

14. A higher solute concentration results in a more negative water potential.

✔ True 正確

ψ decreases as solute concentration increases.

15. Osmosis occurs only in plant cells.

✘ False 錯誤

Osmosis occurs in both plant and animal cells.

Osmosis · T/F 16–20

True / False Set 4 — ✔ if correct · ✘ on wrong words

16. The presence of a solute increases the water potential.

✘ False 錯誤

Solute lowers water potential.

17. Water potential is equal in all solutions.

✘ False 錯誤

ψ depends on solute concentration and pressure.

18. Osmosis cannot occur without a membrane.

✔ True 正確

A differentially permeable membrane is required.

19. Solute concentration does not affect osmosis.

✘ False 錯誤

Solute concentration determines water potential and driving force.

20. Osmosis involves the movement of solute particles.

✘ False 錯誤

Only water moves net-wise in osmosis.
